How I build a revenue engine

Order matters: we don't automate a process nobody has adopted.

  1. Clear processes that teams actually adopt.For you: everyone knows what to do at each step, and does it.
  2. A common language across marketing, sales and customer success.For you: a lead, an opportunity, a customer mean the same thing to everyone.
  3. Digitized actions, on tools that support the processes.For you: analyzable data that shows where it works and where it breaks.
  4. Automation and AI to replace low-value tasks.For you: a faster pace, and time given back to the team for what needs judgment.
